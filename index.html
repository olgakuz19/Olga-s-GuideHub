💼 Recruiter Insider

What recruiters actually
want to see.

After 15+ years on the hiring side of the table, I know exactly what makes candidates stand out — and what sends them to the "no" pile.

🎯

The first 30 seconds matter most

Recruiters form an impression almost immediately. Your opening — how you sit, how you greet, how you start your "tell me about yourself" — sets the entire tone. We script and practice yours until it's effortless.

📋

Your resume is a conversation starter

A resume doesn't get you the job — it gets you the interview. I'll help you write one that sparks curiosity, not confusion, and positions your international experience as an asset, not a question mark.

🗣️

Silence is not weakness

Many candidates rush to fill silence with filler words. Taking a breath before you answer signals confidence. I'll train you to pause with purpose — a skill that separates good candidates from memorable ones.

✉️

Follow-up is a hidden advantage

Most candidates don't send a thoughtful follow-up email. The ones who do stay top of mind. I'll help you craft a thank-you note that keeps you in the conversation — even after you've left the room.

🔍

Research like an insider

Knowing the company's mission statement isn't enough. I show you how to find what actually matters to your interviewer — from recent news to team culture — so your answers feel tailored, not generic.

💬

Ask better questions

"Do you have any questions for us?" is your final impression. Most candidates waste it. I'll coach you on questions that show strategic thinking and leave interviewers genuinely impressed.
