The approach

You don't need more advice.
You need the right strategy.

I have hired hundreds of people. I know what separates the candidates who get offers from the ones who don't, and it is rarely what you think.

  1. 01

    Find out what is actually blocking you

    Most people know something isn't working. Few know what exactly. In our first session, we pinpoint the real issue, whether it is your resume, positioning, interview delivery, or mindset.

  2. 02

    Build a strategy that fits your story

    Your background is not a liability. It is your edge. Whether you are an immigrant, a career changer, or simply undervalued, we build a narrative that works for you.

  3. 03

    Practice like it is the real thing

    Mock interviews, live feedback, and real questions from real hiring managers. We repeat until your answers feel natural, because on the day, confidence is everything.

  4. 04

    Navigate the US market as an immigrant

    The US job market has unwritten rules about communication style, self promotion, and negotiation. I will teach you what nobody else will tell you.

  5. 05

    Sessions in your language

    English, Ukrainian, or Russian, whatever helps you think most clearly. Language should never be a barrier to getting the help you need.

  6. 06

    Support between sessions

    Got an interview tomorrow? Offer came in? I am reachable. Coaching doesn't stop when the call ends. We work through real situations as they happen.

FAQ

Questions everyone asks
before they book.

How much does a session cost?

The first 15 minute call is always free. That is where we figure out what you need. After that, I will recommend a session or package that fits your situation, and you will know the exact price before you commit to anything. No surprises.

Can you guarantee I will get the job or pass my USCIS interview?

No one can honestly promise that, and you should be careful with anyone who does. What I can promise: you will walk in prepared, knowing what to expect, with answers you have practiced and documents in order. Preparation is the part you can control, and we will control it completely.

Are you a lawyer? Is this legal advice?

No. I am not an attorney, and I don't give legal advice. I am a USCIS interpreter with a master's degree in interpreting and translation, and I have sat in hundreds of real interviews. I help you prepare for the experience: the questions, the documents, the nerves. For legal questions about your case, you need an immigration attorney, and I will tell you when that is the case.

Is everything we discuss confidential?

Completely. Your situation, your documents, your story. Nothing leaves our conversation. I never share client information with anyone.

Which language will the session be in?

Your choice. English, Ukrainian, or Russian, whatever helps you think most clearly. Many clients mix languages mid session, and that is perfectly fine.

I am not an immigrant. Can you still help me?

Absolutely. Resume reviews, interview prep, career strategy, and negotiation coaching work the same for everyone. My 15+ years inside tech recruiting is the part that helps you. The immigrant experience is just a bonus for those who need it.
