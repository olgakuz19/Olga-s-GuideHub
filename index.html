❓ FAQ

Questions everyone asks
before they book.

How much does a session cost?

The first 15-minute call is always free — that's where we figure out what you need. After that, I'll recommend a session or package that fits your situation, and you'll know the exact price before you commit to anything. No surprises.

Can you guarantee I'll get the job or pass my USCIS interview?

No one can honestly promise that — and you should be careful with anyone who does. What I can promise: you will walk in prepared, knowing what to expect, with answers you've practiced and documents in order. Preparation is the part you can control, and we'll control it completely.

Are you a lawyer? Is this legal advice?

No — I'm not an attorney, and I don't give legal advice. I'm a USCIS interpreter with a master's degree in interpreting and translation, and I've sat in hundreds of real interviews. I help you prepare for the experience: the questions, the documents, the nerves. For legal questions about your case, you need an immigration attorney — and I'll tell you when that's the case.

Is everything we discuss confidential?

Completely. Your situation, your documents, your story — nothing leaves our conversation. I never share client information with anyone.

Which language will the session be in?

Your choice — English, Ukrainian, or Russian. Whatever helps you think most clearly. Many clients mix languages mid-session, and that's perfectly fine.

I'm not an immigrant — can you still help me?

Absolutely. Resume reviews, interview prep, career strategy, and negotiation coaching work the same for everyone. My 15+ years inside tech recruiting is the part that helps you — the immigrant experience is just a bonus for those who need it.
